GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

Still wondering about something? Send me a message and we'll talk it through.

How much does a website cost?+

Every project is different. I'll review the details and confirm a quote before any work begins. This website does not display invented or automatically calculated prices.

How long will my website take?+

Timing depends on the scope, features and how quickly content and feedback are ready. Share your ideal timeline and we'll agree on a realistic schedule.

Can I request changes during the project?+

Yes. We'll agree on review points and how revisions are handled as part of your project scope before work starts.

Do you provide hosting and domain names?+

Hosting and domains can be discussed during planning. Ownership, provider costs and ongoing responsibilities will be made clear before anything is set up.

What kinds of websites do you build?+

Business, restaurant, e-commerce, portfolio, startup, landing page, organization and custom websites, as well as redesigns.

Can I pay online on this website?+

Online payment processing is not currently connected. Payment milestones and accepted methods are agreed directly before the project begins.
